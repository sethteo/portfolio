A useful technical note does not have to cover an entire subject. Sometimes one problem, one observation, and one example are enough.

## Start with a question

What was confusing? What did you try? What would have helped you understand the problem sooner? Those questions give a short post a clear shape.

- Describe the problem and why it matters.
- Show the smallest example that explains it.
- Finish with what you learned or would try next.
